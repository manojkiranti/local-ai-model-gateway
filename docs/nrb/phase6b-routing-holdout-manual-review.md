### Why native-2 mistakes these for glyph-mapped text

Two of native-2's own signal corrections are defeated by the same typographic habit:

1. **Numbered outline labels.** `5.2.Pension & Gratuity Fund` is one token to the tokenizer, and the intra-word `.` between digits and a capitalised word looks exactly like the intra-word symbol that marks Preeti. §13.2's correction exempts letter-bearing compounds such as `FIU-Nepal` and `F/Y`, but a `<digit>.<digit>.<Word>` label is not one.
2. **Accounting abbreviations.** `A/c`, `P/L`, `B/S` carry a symbol inside a two-letter token with no vowel, which is the vowel-less rule's target shape.

The rest of each sheet is numeric, so those cells are `unjudged` and leave the ratio, shrinking the denominator until a handful of labels lifts it to ~0.5. That is the same small-denominator effect `MIN_JUDGED_FOR_RATIO` was introduced for — it bounds it, it does not eliminate it.

## 10. Evaluation & Improvement

**Success metric.** Share of `>=0.80`-routed NRB blobs whose converted Unicode a Nepali reader marks `confirmed_correct`. Proxy until reviews land: routing precision on unseen files (currently 56/56).

**Eval.** This pack: 56 labelled review items with the flagged unit, the converted output and the rendered source page. Scored by reader verdict per item. Current agreement rate: **not yet measurable** — 0 of 56 adjudicated.

**Feedback capture.** The reader edits the verdict column in §3 and the per-item line in §4, in place, in this file under version control. Disagreements with the `E` (English) column are the routing-precision correction signal; disagreements on converted text are the conversion signal. The two are logged separately because they have different fixes.

**Review loop.** Re-examine when the reader returns verdicts, and otherwise at each extractor-version change. A `native-3` addressing §5 invalidates this cohort as validation evidence and requires a new draw (new seed, `exclude_keys` covering **both** Phase 6A and this holdout).
