--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
G. A DEFECT NATIVE-2 INTRODUCED, AND THE BENCHMARK CAUGHT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Marking uninformative units `unjudged` is correct, and it shrinks the
denominator. In the first native-2 run that flagged SIX documents on the strength
of a single legacy unit out of three or four judged (5504d7f5eb74, e7d44c7f04c0,
917712da7765, 834f70add6e7, 333cad7d028a, 8a57093ed457) — a ratio over four units
is not a measurement. native-1 never saw this because its denominator included
every numeric row.

Fixed with a floor (`MIN_JUDGED_FOR_RATIO = 8`, or `MIN_LEGACY_ABSOLUTE = 4` so a
short wholly-Preeti document still flags), the pass was re-run from scratch, and
all six are clean. Recorded here rather than quietly corrected, because it is the
kind of defect a rule change introduces and only a benchmark finds.
