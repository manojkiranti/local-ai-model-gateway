*** THE FALSE NEGATIVE, AND IT IS SYSTEMATIC ***
8df7b02f8a13 is a SPREADSHEET (236,905 chars) whose content is Preeti-encoded
Nepali ("Plss[t jflif{s cfly{s ultljlw @)&^/&&"), legacy_line_ratio 0.2204 over
345/1565 judged lines — and it is classified `extracted` / `clean`.
Cause: `quality.classify` judges spreadsheets STRUCTURALLY (are there cells?)
and returns before any linguistic rule is consulted, by design, because prose
rules misfire on statistical tables.  The consequence is that legacy-font
Nepali inside a workbook is invisible to the detector.  60 of the 400 benchmark
files are spreadsheets; 44 of them were parsed and all 44 came back `clean`.
This is the dangerous direction, and it is a rule gap, not a threshold.

NO THRESHOLD WAS CHANGED.  legacy_line_ratio >= 0.20 is untouched, and no
classifier edit was made from this benchmark.  Per the plan, a real change
requires bumping EXTRACTOR_VERSION to native-2, re-running over this same frozen
manifest, and reporting native-1 and native-2 side by side.  The two findings
above are the evidence for such a proposal; making it is a separate, reviewed
decision and is deliberately NOT part of this run.
